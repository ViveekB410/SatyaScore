# Round 3 — KG relational pilot: pre-registration

**Written and committed before any answer is generated or any claim is labelled.** Nothing below
may change after results are seen; any deviation is reported as a deviation.

## Why
The PRD calls the KG ablation "the single most important experiment". On the existing 456 gold
claims it cannot be measured: none contains a relation the KG can link (`results/kg_ablation.md`).
The team can annotate ~60–70 more claims, so this pilot tests KG **in its own domain — relational
facts** — with a scoped claim: "does KG help on relational claims?", not "in general".

## Data
- **Questions:** the 31 fixed English questions in `round3/questions.json` (birthplace, place of
  death, capital, headquarters, founder, location, membership), mixing well-known and moderately
  obscure entities.
- **Answers:** one answer per question from `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` via Groq, with the unchanged
  dataset generator (`dataset_pipeline.response_collector.OpenAICompatibleGenerator`: system
  prompt "Answer factually and concisely. Answer in English.", temperature 0.7).
- **Claims:** extracted with the current `ClaimExtractor`. A claim enters the pilot if it contains a
  relation phrase from `RELATION_TO_PID` (a text rule, applied before any KG lookup). If more than
  70 qualify, 70 are sampled with seed 42.
- **Labels:** two annotators label every claim **blind** (no AI verdict, no scores), Verified /
  Uncertain / Hallucinated, following `annotation_round2/INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
- **Gold for analysis:** claims both annotators label the same, as Verified or Hallucinated.
  Uncertain and disagreements are excluded and reported (counts + the disagreement list).

## Signals (computed by `round3_signals.py`, before labels exist)
The live system's settings: demo corpus, top-3 retrieval, relevance threshold 0.3.
- **NLI:** net score, most decisive of the top-3 passages (as the API).
- **Contradiction:** 1 − P(contradiction) on the top-1 passage (as Phase 3).
- **KG:** the fixed verifier — 1.0 supported, 0.0 contradicted, none if unknown.
- **Fused (the PRD's full CMHSA vs semantic+NLI):** mean of NLI and KG where KG exists, else
  NLI alone (the shipped weights: SemSim 0, NLI and KG 0.5 each, renormalised).

## Hypotheses and decision rules (95% CIs, 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples of claims)
- **H1 — KG ablation (PRD):** AUC(fused) − AUC(NLI-only). KG **helps** if the CI lower bound is
  above 0. Also reported against the PRD metric: Hallucinated-F1 at the shipped 0.35/0.65
  cut-offs, fused − NLI-only, with its CI, compared with the PRD target of +0.03.
- **H2 — KG correctness:** among claims where KG answers, the share where "supported" matches
  Verified and "contradicted" matches Hallucinated, with Wilson CIs; plus KG coverage (the share
  of claims with a KG answer).
- **H3 — contradiction signal (Phase 3 re-test):** AUC(1 − P(contra)) − AUC(net). Adopted only if
  the CI lower bound is above 0.
- **Agreement:** binary and 3-class Cohen's κ between the annotators, with bootstrap CIs.
- If fewer than 10 gold Hallucinated claims result, every AUC/F1 is reported but marked
  "too few positives to interpret", and no hypothesis is declared supported.

## Deviation 1 — model (recorded 2026-09-25, before any answer was generated)
- **What happened:** Groq returned HTTP 404 for `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` ("does not exist or
  you do not have access to it"). No answer was generated by any model before this record.
- **Models available to the key** (`round3_generate.py --list-models`): allam-2-7b,
  canopylabs/orpheus-arabic-saudi, canopylabs/orpheus-v1-english,
  meta-llama/llama-prompt-guard-2-22m, meta-llama/llama-prompt-guard-2-86m,
  openai/gpt-oss-120b, openai/gpt-oss-20b, openai/gpt-oss-safeguard-20b, qwen/qwen3.8-27b,
  whisper-large-v3, whisper-large-v3-turbo. No Llama chat model is available (the Llama
  entries are prompt-guard classifiers).
- **Decision:** answers from **`openai/gpt-oss-120b` and `qwen/qwen3.8-27b`**, one answer per
  question each, claims pooled, then the same filter and 70-claim sample (seed 42). These are
  the two general chat models from different families. Excluded: `gpt-oss-20b` (same family as
  the 120b), `allam-2-7b` (Arabic-focused), and the classifier / speech models. Pooling two
  families follows the PRD's multi-LLM dataset step and addresses the "single LLM" limitation.
- **Processing change:** any `<think>…</think>` reasoning block in an answer is removed before
  claim extraction, so only the answer is fact-checked.
- **Consequence:** round 3 is not from the same model as the 456 existing claims. Its results
  are reported as a separate pilot, not pooled with round 1–2 numbers.
- Everything else in this pre-registration is unchanged.

## Deviation 2 — sensitivity analysis (recorded 2026-09-25, after generation, BEFORE any label)
- **Outcome of generation:** 62 answers (31 per model); 45 relational claims, all used (below the
  70 cap): gpt-oss-120b 18, qwen3.8-27b 27. No `<think>` block appeared in any answer.
- **Found on inspection:** the rule-based coreference resolver mapped "its" to "India" in 3 claims,
  garbling their subjects: `r3_q16_qwen3827b_2`, `r3_q26_qwen3827b_1`, `r3_q17_gptoss120b_0`.
- **Primary analysis unchanged:** all 45 claims, as pre-registered.
- **Declared now, before labelling:** a sensitivity analysis repeating H1–H3 without those 3
  claims. Both are reported; the primary result is the one the pre-registration names.
- **Signals locked (before labelling):** `round3/signals_round3.csv`. A first run showed one KG
  lookup failure recorded as "unlinked" (rate-limited search); `round3_signals.py` now retries
  non-answers (a genuine unknown/unlinked is stable). Re-run: exactly that claim changed
  (unlinked → contradicted); NLI identical. Final KG status: supported 8, contradicted 5,
  unknown 15, unlinked 17 (KG answered 13 of 45).
- **Annotation rules (fixed before labelling):** `round3/ANNOTATION_RULES.md` — decision
  procedure (worst fact decides), containment rule for places, date and "founded by" rules,
  literal reading of oddly worded claims, allowed sources (**no Wikidata**, since KG uses it; no
  AI tools), and required evidence URL and notes. Its worked examples use claims outside the
  pilot. It replaces the round-2 instructions for this round.

## Deviation 3 — AI-assisted labels (recorded 2026-09-26, AFTER labels were returned and analysed)
- **What happened:** the rules forbade AI tools. Both returned sheets had notes with AI-style
  features (quoted text absent from the claim in 5 of A's notes; templated phrasing in both).
  Asked, the annotators confirmed they **used an AI assistant and then checked every verdict
  themselves** against the evidence.
- **Timing, stated honestly:** the pre-registered analysis (`analyze_round3.py`) had already been
  run once when this was recorded. The labels, the analysis and the decision rules were not
  changed after seeing the results.
- **Consequences:**
  1. Labels are described everywhere as **AI-assisted, annotator-verified**, not independent
     human judgements.
  2. κ is not evidence of independent agreement: two annotators checking the output of the same
     kind of tool can agree because of the tool.
  3. The gold standard carries an unknown AI bias. The systems being evaluated are also LLM-based,
     so the direction of that bias cannot be assumed.
  4. H1–H3 are reported as **suggestive, not confirmed**, whatever the intervals show. The
     primary H1 lower bound is +0.003, and the sensitivity analysis (Deviation 2) includes zero.
- **Mitigation available:** a blind, no-AI audit of a random subset by a person who has not seen
  the labels, comparing their verdicts with the gold (`round3/round3_audit_blind.xlsx`, 20 claims,
  seed 7). If the audit is done, its agreement with the gold is reported next to this deviation.

## Exploratory analysis 1 — KG place-linking fix (recorded 2026-09-26, after labels; NOT pre-registered)
- **Diagnosis:** 4 of the 5 KG "contradicted" results came from linking the place to a wrong
  Wikidata item (the top search hit): "Erode" → a given name, "Naigaon" → a monorail station
  (both true birthplaces, wrongly contradicted), "Niphad" → an insect genus, "Bodua" → an
  encyclopedia article (both false claims, contradicted only by luck).
- **Fix:** for place relations (P19, P20, P131, P159) the object is the set of items whose name
  is exactly the claimed place, excluding obvious non-places; supported if any of them is (or
  contains) the true value, contradicted only if none is; no exactly-named place → unlinked.
- **Re-run:** KG only (`round3_kg_fix.py` → `round3/signals_round3_kgfix.csv`,
  `results/round3_kg_fix_exploratory.md`). The locked signals and the pre-registered result are
  unchanged and remain the primary result; the re-run is reported as exploratory.

## Exploratory analysis 2 — the KG decides when it answers (recorded 2026-09-27; NOT pre-registered)
- **Change:** when the KG answers (supported / contradicted), its answer is the claim's score;
  the NLI/KG fusion is used only when it has none (`member1_model/cmhsa.py`, `KG_DECIDES`).
- **Evidence** (`round3_kg_decides.py` → `results/round3_kg_decides.md`, KG signals after
  Exploratory analysis 1): in the 9 gold claims where KG and NLI disagreed by ≥ 0.5, the KG was
  right 9 times. AUC vs 50/50 fusion: 0.848 → 0.872, ΔAUC +0.024 [−0.000, +0.083] (not
  established); Hallucinated-F1 unchanged (0.649).
- **Hindi KG path added:** Hindi relation templates (birth, death, capital, location) with
  exact Hindi-name Wikidata linking; 11/11 live spot checks as expected. It cannot be
  evaluated on existing labels: 0 of 190 Hindi gold claims (and 0 of 456 overall) are relational.
- **Limit:** the KG checks one relation; other facts in the same claim are not checked when it
  decides.
